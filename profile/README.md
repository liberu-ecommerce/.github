## Description

Welcome to **[Liberu Ecommerce](https://www.liberusoftware.com)**, a visionary open-source project by **[Liberu Software](https://www.liberusoftware.com)** that revolutionizes the world of online commerce. Built with Laravel 12, PHP 8.5, Livewire 4, and Filament 5, Liberu Ecommerce is not just a platform for buying and selling — it's an innovative solution designed to provide a seamless and dynamic shopping experience for businesses and consumers alike.

Liberu Ecommerce is open source, released under the permissive MIT license. We invite developers, businesses, and ecommerce enthusiasts to contribute to the evolution of online commerce. Together, let's redefine the standards of ecommerce platforms.

## Key Features

1. **Intuitive Shopping Experience:** A user-friendly shopping interface ensuring customers can browse, select, and purchase products with ease.
2. **Dynamic Livewire Interactions:** Real-time, reactive components for a seamless shopping experience without full page reloads.
3. **Efficient Admin Panel:** Filament-powered admin panel with tools to manage products, orders, inventory, and settings.
4. **Secure Payment Processing:** Support for secure payment gateways to protect customers and businesses.
5. **Customizable Templates:** Flexible design system allowing businesses to tailor their storefront to their brand identity.
6. **Modular Architecture:** Clean, reusable modules promoting code maintainability and extensibility.
7. **Responsive Design:** Optimized for all devices and screen sizes.
8. **Advanced Search & Filtering:** Empowering users to quickly find products within extensive catalogs.
9. **Multi-language & Multi-currency Support:** Reach a global audience with localization and currency features.
10. **SEO Optimization:** SEO-friendly structure to maximize visibility on search engines.
